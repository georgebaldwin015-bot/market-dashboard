Strategy review

How Edge Lab works and what the evidence says. Rewritten 5 Oct 2026 on the honest backtest: a stock only counts while it was really in the S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100.
In one paragraph: Take A+ and A setups (3+ of the five points below), enter on the buy-stop, stop at 1× ADR, and let the Auto exit take you out. In every kind of market, same plan, same size. Tested honestly over 2017–26, that made about 18% a year with a worst drop of about 26%, against 15% a year and a 34% drop for simply holding the S&P 500. It comes in bursts: excellent in 2020, 2023, 2025 and 2026, flat in 2018, 2019, 2022 and 2024. The biggest gain left on the table is your exits: selling before the rule.

1 · Why the numbers changed

  • The old backtest had hindsight. It tested today's S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100 list back to 2017, so it traded stocks like AppLovin, Lumentum and Super Micro before they joined the index (often because they had soared), when the scanner couldn't have seen them. 72% of the old profit came from those trades.
  • Now: every stock that was in either index since 2016 (770, including ~250 that have since dropped out) is tested, each only while it was a member. Stocks since delisted or taken over (Abiomed, Marathon Oil, Hess) have no price history and are the one gap left.
  • Two over-harsh assumptions were fixed: fill days too old to check on hourly bars count as surviving at the measured rate (77%) instead of all as losses, and open trades count at their latest price.
  • Every result is shown in both halves (2017–22 and 2023–26) and, for accounts, with bad / typical / good luck in which same-day setups got taken (60 shuffles). A rule only counts if it holds in both halves.

2 · What happens every night (UK time is UTC+1 in summer)

  • 21:30 UTC (after the US close): the screener scans ~520 US stocks, finds the ones armed for tomorrow, grades them and publishes the ‘Today’ tab, the Market tab and the email.
  • 22:00 UTC: IBKR sync: fills, open positions and IBKR's daily return; stops for every ADR band and the Auto exit.
  • 22:45 UTC: the forward test updates the live record and grades each of your trades the way ‘Today’ would have.
  • 23:10 UTC: the stop email: tomorrow's Auto stops, what moved, what was hit, what's off plan. Nothing is ever sent to IBKR.

3 · The setups

  • Two strategies: Minervini (trend template: Stage 2 uptrend above rising averages) and QM (Qullamaggie-style momentum: ADR 3%+, RS 80+, above the 10/20-day EMA).
  • Entry: a buy-stop at the 20-day highest close. It only fills if the stock opens below it and trades up through it; a gap straight over is no trade.
  • Stop: 1× ADR below the buy-stop. That distance is "1R".
  • Size: shares = 1% of the account ÷ (buy-stop − stop). With a 1× ADR stop that ties up 20–30% of a cash account per position, so 4–5 positions at a time.

7 · Margin (with caution)

With 1.5× buying power the same trades made about 33% a year (worst drop ~29%); at 2×, about 38% (~34%). Bigger drops, and margin interest isn't included. 1.5× is the cautious step; the With margin panel on the Backtest tab keeps the current figures.

8 · Your trades

  • The exits are the gap. On your 16 closed trades graded A+ to B (old grade), selling before the rule made −12.3R; the same trades held to the Auto exit made +8.9R. Over the last 30 days, early exits cost 13.2R on 3 trades. The Account tab now flags each one and keeps a monthly score.
  • The backtest assumes every exit follows the rule. That's the single biggest thing between it and the account.

9 · The checklist

  • Only A+ and A. The ‘Today’ tab shows the grade and why; B and below are off plan.
  • Enter on the buy-stop, not before and not chasing more than ~1% above.
  • Stop at 1× ADR; size so that stop is 1% of the account. Same in every market.
  • Don't sell a winner by feel. Move the stop to the Auto level each night (the stop email has it) and let it take you out.
  • Check the Discipline panel in Account weekly.

10 · Caveats

  • The five points were chosen after seeing both halves of the history; their cut-offs come from 2017–22 only and they held on two data sets, but live trading is the real test. The forward test records the new grade and the old one side by side.
  • Delisted and taken-over stocks are missing (no free price history).
  • Results use 1% risk and a cash limit on a $10k account; a larger or margin account behaves differently.
  • Account results swing a lot on which same-day setups get taken, so read them as ranges, not single numbers.
