ⓘ Notes

The grade since 5 Oct 2026: one point each for a QM setup, Stage 1 (breaking out of a base), a 5%+ pullback from the 52-week high, ADR 3.3%+ and a share price under $41 -- the five factors that held in both 2017-22 and 2023-26 on the honest backtest (a stock counts only while it was in the S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100) -- plus, since 7 Oct, a shakeout while the market is above its 200-day. A+ = 4-6, A = 3, B = 2, C = 1, D = 0; the plan is A+ and A.

What earns a point (each is simply yes or no -- one point, never more; six in all):

  • QM setup -- the Qullamaggie scan found it (Minervini setups get no point).
  • Stage 1 -- breaking out of a flat base rather than an established uptrend.
  • 5% or more below the 52-week high -- any pullback of 5%+ counts the same, so 12% and 30% both get the point. On the honest backtest every depth from 5% to 40% beat setups right at the high (about +0.2R a trade on Auto), with 10-40% best; no depth did worse, so there's no upper limit.
  • ADR 3.3% or more -- the stock moves at least 3.3% a day on average.
  • Share price under $41 -- at the buy-stop.
  • Shakeout -- in the 10 days before, it dipped under its 20-day low and closed back above it (top half of the day), while the market was above its 200-day average. In a falling market a dip under the lows is usually real selling, so it doesn't count then.

ⓘ Notes

On each chart: solid blue = pivot (20-day highest close), red dashed = 1x ADR stop, triangles = entry and the Auto exit -- the exit the app uses: the 4x ADR band, widened to 6x when the market is 5%+ above its 200-day average at entry and 8x at 10%+ (the trade's Auto band is the solid line). The lines are your TradingView ADR Trailing Bands (close minus 2/4/6/8 x the 20-day average high-low range): 2, 4, 6, 8. An X in a band's colour marks where following that band would have sold (1x ADR stop, breakeven at +2R, then raise the stop to the band at each close, never lower); its R is in the legend and under the chart.

Examples below are picked at random from closed 2025-26 trades (not the best ones), so expect losers in every grade. A grade is about the odds over many trades, not any single chart. The points were chosen with both halves of the history in view (cut-offs from 2017-22 only); live recs from 5 Oct 2026 onward are the real test.
